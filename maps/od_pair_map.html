Corridor
Steady-state physics only (no momentum/coasting) -- a short, steep pinch can show every locomotive as infeasible here even on an otherwise easy corridor, since a train's real momentum through a brief climb isn't credited. See Zurich-Basel for a real example: a single 2.7% pinch near Zürich HB, well within any of these locomotives' adhesion limit individually, but combined with the full 1,600t train weight it exceeds it under this static solver.
