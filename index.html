Corrected 2026-09-08, late evening. A routing error was found and fixed: the model was routing traffic over a real chokepoint (Kúty/Brodské, SK/CZ border, 5.0% gradient) that real cargo traffic avoids in practice. Excluding it and rerunning the full 254-country-pair analysis moved EURO9000's headline win-share from 16.0% down to 4.1% — 74.5% of its prior win-share was resting on that one segment. The headline table, the 6-corridor analysis, and the corridor maps below are now corrected. The network winner map, the two gradient maps, and the OD-pair route picker below still predate this fix (the gradient maps are unaffected by it — they don't depend on cost/routing choices; the network map and OD-pair picker do, and are queued for a refresh).
